# Reminiscence Group PWA (Hong Kong Elderly Home)

A facilitator-led PWA for 30-minute group reminiscence sessions in an elderly home. The same app runs with feature parity on a laptop or mobile and is mirrored to a TV; residents participate aloud while the therapist controls the screen.

## Language

**Facilitator Surface**:
The single app instance (Windows/Mac laptop or iOS/Android mobile) that drives the TV through HDMI mirroring. Laptop and mobile expose the same features, and only the therapist touches it.
_Avoid_: controller, remote control

**Mirror Mode**:
The TV displays exactly what the facilitator surface shows; there is no second-screen sync.
_Avoid_: casting, extended desktop

**Group Session**:
A 30-minute activity for 8-12 residents with mild-to-moderate dementia, led by one therapist.
_Avoid_: class, workshop

**Resident**:
A group participant who is never expected to touch a device.
_Avoid_: patient, user, player

**Session Plan**:
A saved sequence of media cards organized into reorderable phases (Warm-up, Songs + Photos, Video, Recall, Discussion, Close).
_Avoid_: playlist, slideshow

**Theme**:
An organizing category such as Toys, Living Environment, or Jobs, used to group media and question pools.
_Avoid_: topic

**Media Item**:
A song, photo, short video clip, or text/caption card used as the focus of reminiscence, carrying title, source credit, year, place, theme, caption, and note fields.

**Media Card**:
The full-screen presentation of one media item in a session. Cards appear in a linear sequence and are not coupled to each other.

**Recall Question**:
A factual multiple-choice question with one correct answer and 2-4 choices, revealed gently by the therapist.
_Avoid_: trivia, quiz question

**Discussion Prompt**:
An open-ended question with no single right answer, intended to generate shared memories.
_Avoid_: conversation starter

**Question Overlay**:
The on-demand panel opened from a media card, showing recall questions first and discussion prompts after.

**Content Library**:
The collection of media items and questions imported into the app on a device.

**Starter Library**:
The initial collection compiled during development: 30 songs, 120 photos, short video clips, text/caption cards, and 10 starter session plans. The running app never fetches or searches for content on the web, and the home already holds licences.
_Avoid_: live content feed

**Source Credit**:
An optional provenance or credit field on a media item; commercial files are provider-supplied, and other items come from legitimate public or openly licensed sources.

**Full-Track Playback**:
Songs play in full by default, with progress, skip, and Repeat Song controls.

**Online-First Delivery**:
Media is loaded from hosted storage by default, and a session can be prepared for offline use ahead of time.

**Prepared Offline Session**:
A session whose media and questions have been downloaded to the device before the group starts.

**Session Log**:
The post-group record of date, theme, engagement level, attendance, notes, and notable memories, exportable for home records.

**Standard Written Chinese Screen Language**:
On-screen text uses standard written Chinese in Traditional characters; spoken facilitation remains Cantonese.

**Visible Control Bar**:
The on-TV navigation bar with Back, Next, Repeat Song, Reveal Answer, and Home controls.

**Explore Grid**:
The filtered library view used outside sessions, organized by Theme, Decade, and Place.

**Content Pack**:
A single-file import package containing new media items, questions, and optional session plans, installed manually by the home.


**Phase**:
A labelled section of a session plan (Warm-up, Songs + Photos, Video, Recall, Discussion, Close); phases are reorderable.
